# Tuning anchors

Every balance number lives in `Assets/StreamingAssets/Data/tuning.json` (plus the per-record
tables in `vehicles.json`, `attachments.json`, `lifts.json`, `guests.json`, `economy.json`,
`climate.json`, `construction.json`, `stations.json`, `tasks.json`, `operators.json`). Keys are
dotted paths read in code through `ctx.Tuning.F("group.key")`, `.I(...)` or `.Curve(key, x)`;
a missing key throws, so there are no silent fallbacks in C#.

Each anchor carries its allowed range and the real-world basis it was taken from. This file is
generated by `python3 tools/gen_tuning_doc.py`; edit `tuning.json`, not this document.

## `simulation`

| Key | Value | Range | Unit | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `simulation.resortOpenHour` | 9 | 8 .. 10 | h | Lifts open; day mode begins. Typical alpine resort 8:30-9:00. |
| `simulation.resortCloseHour` | 16 | 15 .. 17 | h | Lifts close; night mode (vehicle operation) begins after sweep. |
| `simulation.nightShiftStartHour` | 17 | 16 .. 19 | h | Grooming may start once patrol sweep is done. |
| `simulation.guestsPerAgent` | 4 | 1 .. 50 | guests | Cohort size: one simulated agent represents this many guests. 1 = every guest individual. Tests raise it for speed. |
| `simulation.guestTickDivisor` | 4 | 1 .. 10 | ticks | Guest cohorts step every Nth tick with N times the step: skiing, walking and queueing at 5 Hz is indistinguishable from 20 Hz and costs a quarter as much; the snow, lifts and machines still tick at 20 Hz. |
| `simulation.aiVehicleTickDivisor` | 1 | 1 .. 20 | ticks | AI-driven vehicles integrate every N ticks with dt*N (LOD). Player vehicle always 1. |
